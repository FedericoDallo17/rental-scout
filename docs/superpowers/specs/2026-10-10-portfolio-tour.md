# Public portfolio tour

Provide `/tour` for recruiters without requiring an account. Use explicitly fictional,
in-memory examples rather than a shared demo login or production inventory. Walk through
saved searches, currency-aware matching, personal tracking, notifications, and inventory
health. Allow visitors to change a sample budget, favorite an apartment, and preview an
alert. Nothing persists or triggers delivery, acquisition, or browser permissions.

Use the existing workbench palette and system type, a left-aligned guide beside the
sample product surface, navy (#17324d), slate (#425b70), white, pale blue (#edf3f7),
and the app's existing borders. Steps form a real sequence; avoid marketing statistics.
Support English/Spanish, keyboard navigation, mobile layouts, and readable content
without JavaScript. Link from sign-in and signed-in navigation.
