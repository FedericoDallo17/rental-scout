# Timbre tour entrance

User-approved scope: make `/tour` visually compelling with a growing, centered
brand symbol, ringing circles, and a click-triggered door opening that reveals
the existing Timbre lettering before the five-step journey begins.

Use the existing Patio palette (green #173F36, ivory #F4F0E6, yellow #F2CA52,
background #F3F5EC, text #17312A) and local Manrope. A full-screen brand stage
leads into an open chapter layout: quiet top navigation, horizontal step index,
large headings, and functional sample controls. The logo is the memorable moment.
Reuse approved D2 geometry rather than drawing another mark. Keep sample data,
private routes, and bilingual behavior. No new dependency or backend behavior.

Keyboard users can activate or skip the entrance. Reduced motion enters without
waiting for animation. Direct chapter links bypass the entrance; without JS all
chapters remain readable. Turbo caching must not strand users behind the intro.
