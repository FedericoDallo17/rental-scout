# Ticket: 033 - Aggregate Feed Controls

## Goal

Let users browse and refine the deduplicated union of all saved-search matches, while
retaining focused single-search views.

## Acceptance Criteria

- With no selected search, the feed shows the union of all saved-search matches once.
- Clicking the active search clears it and returns to the aggregate feed.
- Aggregate cards identify every saved search they match.
- Controls sort by newest, price, known total monthly cost, or fit.
- Controls filter by source, neighborhood, currency, and known expenses.
- Search counts and aggregate membership are calculated without query-per-search execution.
- “All fresh inventory” remains distinct and out of scope.
