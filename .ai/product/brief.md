# Product Brief: Rental Scout Lite

## Idea
A lightweight apartment scout that scrapes rental sources, ranks listings against preferences, and shows all scraped apartments in one portal.

## Target Location
Montevideo, Uruguay.

## Initial Target User
A person looking for an apartment who wants one place to review new relevant listings.

## Core Direction
- Hourly scraping
- Multi-source ingestion
- Listing normalization
- Deduplication
- Match scoring
- Notification events
- Portal/dashboard review
